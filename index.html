About

I build machine learning systems where the guarantee matters as much as the accuracy.

My research combines differential privacy, secure aggregation and federated learning into distributed-DP systems with end-to-end, per-client guarantees and no trusted server. I also work on second-order optimisation that wins back the utility DP-SGD loses (first author, ICML 2026).

I've worked across industry and healthcare. At CERN I build privacy-enhancing technologies into the CAFEIN federated learning platform. Before that I built generative-model pipelines and a radiologist evaluation platform at Kantonsspital Aarau, clinical-trial data tooling at Roche, and stroke decision support at Vall d'Hebron. I'm also the technical co-founder of Kosmico.

Research
ICML 2026 · first author

DP-KFC: data-free preconditioning for private deep learning

A network's optimisation geometry comes from its architecture, not its data. Probing with structured synthetic noise builds KFAC preconditioners that beat DP-SGD and adaptive baselines at ε ≤ 3 across vision and language, with no private or public data.

KFAC · Mean-field theory · Pink noise→ PoPETs · under review

The Missing Sampler: distributed privacy accounting for async FL

Buffered async FL lets fast clients accumulate up to 97× the reported privacy loss. A privatiser committee restores per-client accounting and subsampling amplification without a trusted server or TEE, audited with LiRA-style membership inference and canaries at 10³–10⁴ clients.

Distributed DP · Secure aggregation→ Applied · deployed

Synthetic data hospitals can actually share

Text-conditioned diffusion, GAN, VQ-/RQ-VAE and autoregressive models on ~5,000 image–report pairs, judged by five radiologists across ~2,500 samples for quality, diversity and memorisation.
